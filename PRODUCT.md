# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS: one self-contained page, no build step, no runtime dependencies.

## Users

Owners and managers of small local businesses (services, retail, food) who need to attract and retain customers but have no in-house marketing team.

## Product Purpose

A one-page site for a marketing consultancy. It explains the offer, earns trust, and leads to booking a diagnostic call. Success is a booked call or a sent inquiry.

## Positioning

Marketing for a local business to become "the plan": clarity before noise. The page interprets the `.party` domain as a neighborhood celebration, but it must not claim a proprietary method, guaranteed results, or measured outcomes.

## Operating Context

Read quickly on a phone, often outside working hours, while comparing local agencies and freelancers. Expected next step: a form, email, or WhatsApp message.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single page with anchor navigation.
- Spanish copy.
- No backend: the contact step must resolve through mailto, WhatsApp, or an equivalent that actually works. No invented endpoint.
- No required third-party runtime dependencies: the typeface is self-hosted and embedded.
- Contact address: `jonathan@shuaaay.party` (confirmed by the owner).

## Brand Commitments

Name: Shuaaay. Domain: shuaaay.party. The domain sets a festive, neighborhood tone; the page interprets it as a street-party poster. No logo or palette has been supplied; the current system is a design proposal.

## Evidence on Hand

None: no cases, testimonials, metrics, pricing, or real clients. The page must not fabricate them, and it states that absence explicitly. Replace the honesty note once real proof exists.

## Product Principles

- Clarity over hype; the design may celebrate, the promises may not.
- Speak to a business owner, not to a CMO.
- Every section answers a real buying question.
- Show concrete work detail instead of invented numbers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target: WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, visible focus, keyboard navigation, and respect for prefers-reduced-motion.
